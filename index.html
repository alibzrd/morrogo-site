What we do

Two ways we work, one standard

Private or corporate, the approach is the same: we listen closely, we design something specific to you, and we stay accountable for it from start to finish.

01

Tailor-Made Private Experiences

Every trip begins with a conversation, not a catalogue. We listen to what you actually want, then design an experience around it. Creativity and rigour are what we bring to the table; the brief is yours.

  • Built entirely around your brief
  • Hotels, private villas & riads, drivers, guides
  • Planned and delivered by the same team
The difficult requests

The ones other agencies quietly decline are usually the ones worth doing. Tell us what you have in mind, however specific, and we will tell you honestly whether it can be done.

02

Corporate Events & Delegations

Conferences, off-sites, retreats, incentives, or full logistics for a visiting delegation — we design the format around your objective and run it on the ground, at the scale you need.

  • Conferences, off-sites & retreats
  • Delegation logistics, end to end
  • Venue, transport & accommodation
Business access

When your visit has a commercial purpose, we open doors — introductions to Moroccan companies, institutions and sector partners, arranged around your agenda. Few agencies can do this. We can.

Behind both is the same foundation: a broad network of vetted partners across Morocco — hotels and private residences, riads and camps, transport, guides, venues and activity providers. We select from it case by case, so what you get is chosen for your brief rather than for our convenience.

This is not an exhaustive list. If it happens in Morocco, start the conversation with us — we will tell you honestly whether we are the right team for it.

Our pricing principle

You see what everything costs

We negotiate hard with our partners, and we pass those rates on to you as they are. Every line of your budget is visible: what the hotel charges, what the transport costs, what a guide is paid. Nothing is bundled into an opaque total.

Clients are often surprised to find that this comes in below what a conventional agency would quote for the same programme. We think that is simply what transparency looks like when it is applied properly.

Who we are

A Casablanca team, not a booking platform

MorroGo was founded on a simple conviction: Morocco is worth more than a standard package. We work in small numbers, deliberately, so that the person who takes your first call is the same one answering when something needs solving at 7am on day three.

We know the country because we live and work here, and we rely on a network of partners we have chosen ourselves — hoteliers, drivers, guides, venues, private hosts. Those relationships are direct, which means honest advice on what is worth your time and no distance between the promise and what actually happens.

"We don't sell Morocco from a brochure. We plan it from where we live, so every detail holds up on the ground."
Abdou Kabbaj — Founder, MorroGo
